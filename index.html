🧠 What is Rakshya App?

Rakshya is a comprehensive women's safety Android app with a self-hosted backend. It provides fast emergency assistance through SOS alerts, live GPS location sharing, encrypted video capture, and quick trusted-contact notification. All sensitive data is encrypted on-device with AES-256-GCM, and the backend stores only opaque encrypted blobs.

🛠️ Technologies Used

Rakshya is built using modern technologies:

  • Kotlin 2.0 + Jetpack Compose (Android UI)
  • Node.js + TypeScript + Express (Backend)
  • Hilt 2.52 (Dependency Injection)
  • SQLite via node:sqlite (Database)
  • Google Credential Manager (Authentication)
  • AES-256-GCM encryption (Android Keystore)
  • CameraX (Video Capture)
  • OkHttp (Networking)

This makes it a secure, self-hosted safety platform with on-device encryption.

⚡ How Does the SOS Feature Work?

When activated, Rakshya:

  • Sends an emergency alert
  • Shares the user’s live location
  • Notifies pre-selected contacts instantly

This ensures quick response in critical situations.

🚧 Challenges During Development

Building a real-time safety app comes with challenges:

  • Migrating from a broken Supabase-backed architecture to a self-hosted backend
  • Implementing on-device AES-256-GCM encryption with Android Keystore
  • Google sign-in via Credential Manager with proper token exchange
  • Foreground service lifecycle management for SOS and location tracking
  • Keeping the UI simple and responsive under stress conditions

Q / A

Frequently asked questions about Rakshya

What is Rakshya app?

Rakshya is a comprehensive women's safety Android app with a self-hosted Node.js backend. It provides SOS alerts, live GPS tracking, encrypted video capture, emergency contacts, ride monitoring, check-ins, safe places, legal help, and fake call—all with on-device AES-256-GCM encryption.

Why is a women safety app important?

In unsafe situations, response speed matters. Rakshya is designed to reduce response time and make emergency communication immediate.

How does SOS work in Rakshya?

SOS activates a synchronized emergency workflow: it creates an incident on the backend, records SOS-flagged GPS locations every 30 seconds, triggers an emergency call to 112, and shows a full-screen alarm notification—all in one tap.

Who can use Rakshya?

While designed with women safety in mind, Rakshya can also support students, travelers, night shift workers, and anyone seeking personal safety assistance.

What technologies power the app?

Kotlin 2.0 with Jetpack Compose for the Android UI, Node.js + TypeScript + Express for the backend, Hilt for dependency injection, SQLite for local data, Google Credential Manager for authentication, and AES-256-GCM encryption via Android Keystore for on-device security.

What challenges are involved in development?

Migrating from a broken Supabase-backed architecture to a self-hosted backend, implementing on-device AES-256-GCM encryption with Android Keystore, Google sign-in via Credential Manager with proper token exchange, and managing foreground service lifecycles for SOS and location tracking.

What future upgrades are planned?

v1.2: Restore SOS screen and location tracking. v1.3: Restore ride monitoring and check-ins. v1.4: Restore emergency contacts and video capture. v1.5: Restore safe places, legal help, and fake call. v2.0: Production hardening, release signing, and Play Store submission.

How does voice-triggered SOS work?

Rakshya can listen for a distress phrase you configure and start the emergency workflow when speaking is safer than reaching for the phone—so response can begin even when your hands are not free.

Does Rakshya record audio or video during an emergency?

Yes. Rakshya captures video using CameraX and encrypts it on-device with AES-256-GCM before backing up encrypted bytes to the backend. The server never sees plaintext—only opaque encrypted blobs. The encrypted video can be downloaded and decrypted on-device.

How does Rakshya protect SOS and incident data?

All sensitive data is encrypted on-device with AES-256-GCM using keys stored in the Android Keystore. The backend stores only opaque encrypted blobs and never has access to plaintext, so your data stays protected even during emergency delivery.

What is ride monitoring or route deviation?

During trips, Rakshya tracks your live route against an expected path using Haversine distance calculations. If your movement deviates off-course, it detects the risk in real time—useful for rides, commutes, and travel where detours matter.

How do safety check-ins work?

You schedule safety check-ins through CheckInService, a foreground service that prompts you to confirm you are safe. If you miss a check-in, your trusted contacts can be notified automatically to reduce uncertainty.

Is Rakshya a replacement for calling emergency services (for example 100 or 911)?

No. Rakshya is a companion for alerting people you trust and sharing location—it does not replace police, ambulance, or official emergency lines. Use local emergency numbers when life is in immediate danger.
