Privacy by Architecture

Built on serious security, not promises.

Rakshyaa encrypts your contacts, location history, and videos on-device with AES-256-GCM, with keys stored in the Android Keystore. Optional cloud backups contain only this encrypted data. You can use every safety tool without an account; signing in adds backup and incident reporting.

How it works

Your data stays encrypted on your device.

01

Encrypt on device

Contacts, location history, and videos are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, with keys stored in the Android Keystore.

02

Back up only ciphertext

If you turn on backup, only encrypted files are uploaded. Your account profile and SOS incident reports are stored in readable form so they can be used.

03

Secure cloud

Backend on Cloudflare Workers with a D1 database. Encrypted files are stored privately on Cloudinary. See the Privacy Policy.

🧠 What is Rakshyaa App?

Rakshyaa is a women's safety Android app. It helps you reach trusted contacts quickly with an SOS countdown that prepares messages and calls, and it offers location tracking and encrypted video capture. Contacts, location history, and videos are encrypted on-device with AES-256-GCM, and optional cloud backups contain only encrypted data.

🛠️ Technologies Used

Rakshyaa is built using modern technologies:

  • Kotlin 2.0 + Jetpack Compose (Android UI)
  • Cloudflare Workers + Hono + TypeScript (Backend)
  • Hilt (Dependency Injection)
  • Cloudflare D1 (Database) and Cloudinary (encrypted file storage)
  • Google Credential Manager (Authentication)
  • AES-256-GCM encryption (Android Keystore)
  • CameraX (Video Capture)
  • OkHttp (Networking)

Sensitive data is encrypted on-device before anything is backed up.

⚡ How Does the SOS Feature Work?

When activated, Rakshyaa:

  • Starts a countdown you can cancel
  • Records your location on the device
  • Prepares an SMS with your location and a call for your trusted contacts, which you send yourself
  • When you are signed in, reports the incident to our backend

Rakshyaa does not send messages or place calls without your action.

🚧 Challenges During Development

Building a real-time safety app comes with challenges:

  • Migrating from a broken Supabase-backed architecture to a Cloudflare Workers backend
  • Implementing on-device AES-256-GCM encryption with Android Keystore
  • Google sign-in via Credential Manager with proper token exchange
  • Foreground service lifecycle management for SOS and location tracking
  • Keeping the UI simple and responsive under stress conditions

🔮 Future Improvements

Planned upgrades for Rakshyaa include:

Q / A

Frequently asked questions about Rakshyaa

What is Rakshyaa app?

Rakshyaa is a women's safety Android app. It provides an SOS countdown, GPS tracking, encrypted video capture, emergency contacts, ride monitoring, check-ins, safe places, legal help, and fake call, with on-device AES-256-GCM encryption.

Why is a women safety app important?

In unsafe situations, response speed matters. Rakshyaa is designed to reduce response time and make emergency communication immediate.

How does SOS work in Rakshyaa?

SOS starts a countdown you can cancel. It then records your location on the device and prepares an SMS with your location and a call for your trusted contacts. You choose whether to send them; the app never sends messages or calls on its own. When you are signed in, it also reports the incident to our backend.

Who can use Rakshyaa?

While designed with women safety in mind, Rakshyaa can also support students, travelers, night shift workers, and anyone seeking personal safety assistance.

What technologies power the app?

Kotlin with Jetpack Compose for the Android UI, Cloudflare Workers with Hono and TypeScript for the backend, Cloudflare D1 and Cloudinary for storage, Hilt for dependency injection, Google Credential Manager for sign-in, and AES-256-GCM encryption via Android Keystore for on-device security.

What challenges are involved in development?

Migrating from a broken Supabase-backed architecture to a Cloudflare Workers backend, implementing on-device AES-256-GCM encryption with Android Keystore, Google sign-in via Credential Manager with proper token exchange, and managing foreground service lifecycles for SOS and location tracking.

What future upgrades are planned?

A public Google Play release, check-in timers that survive restarts, and retries for incident reports and video backups.

Does Rakshyaa record video during an emergency?

Yes, when you start a recording. Rakshyaa captures video with CameraX and encrypts it on-device with AES-256-GCM. If cloud backup is on, only the encrypted file is uploaded. Recording does not start automatically.

How does Rakshyaa protect SOS and incident data?

Contacts, location history, and videos are encrypted on-device with AES-256-GCM using keys stored in the Android Keystore, and backups contain only encrypted data. SOS incident reports (status, location, and time) are stored readably on our backend so they can be viewed by Rakshyaa operators. See the Privacy Policy.

What is ride monitoring or route deviation?

During a ride, Rakshyaa records your route and compares each new location with the points already recorded. If you move more than 300 metres away from them, it alerts you.

How do safety check-ins work?

You schedule a check-in and the app runs a timer that asks you to confirm you are safe. If you miss it, you get a notification reminding you to message your trusted contacts. Contacts are not messaged automatically, and timers do not yet survive the app being closed by the system or a restart.

Is Rakshyaa a replacement for calling emergency services (for example 100 or 911)?

No. Rakshyaa is a companion for alerting people you trust and sharing location—it does not replace police, ambulance, or official emergency lines. Use local emergency numbers when life is in immediate danger.

Where can I download Rakshyaa?

Rakshyaa is available for Android on Google Play. There is no iOS version.
