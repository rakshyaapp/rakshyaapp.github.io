Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026. This policy explains how the Rakshyaa app collects, protects, and handles your information.

1. Overview

Rakshyaa is a personal safety app designed to help you alert the people you trust and share your location in an emergency. Privacy is built into the app's architecture: sensitive data is encrypted on-device before it ever leaves your phone. This policy describes what information the app collects, why it is collected, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information — when you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account name, email address, and profile picture so you can use the app.
  • Profile information — information you choose to add, such as a phone number and bio.
  • Emergency contacts — the contacts you add for emergency notification. These are encrypted on-device.
  • Location data — GPS location while you use location features, background tracking you start, and SOS-flagged locations during an incident.
  • Incident and SOS data — when you are signed in, starting an SOS reports an incident to our backend with its status, location coordinates, and timestamps.
  • Video recordings — recordings you capture as evidence, encrypted on-device. If cloud backup is on, the encrypted file is also backed up.
  • Ride and check-in data — ride routes, route-deviation events, and safety check-ins you schedule.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to provide and operate the app, including: allowing sign-in and backup, preparing SOS messages with your location that you choose to send, recording and encrypting incident evidence, tracking rides and check-ins, discovering nearby safe places, and maintaining the security, safety, and integrity of the service.

4. Encryption and On-Device Protection

Rakshyaa is built on a privacy-by-architecture model:

  • Contacts, location logs, rides, check-ins, notes, and videos are encrypted on-device with AES-256-GCM.
  • Encryption keys are stored only in the Android Keystore on your device — they never leave your phone.
  • Optional cloud backups contain only this encrypted data. Our servers cannot read their contents.
  • Because keys stay on the device, a backup can only be decrypted by the same installation. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage makes existing backups unreadable.
  • Your account profile (name, email, profile picture, phone, bio), incident reports, and backup file metadata (names, sizes, timestamps) are not end-to-end encrypted and are readable by our backend.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google — Google sign-in via Credential Manager. Google handles authentication and provides basic account details you authorize.
  • Cloudflare — hosts our backend (Cloudflare Workers) and its database (Cloudflare D1), which stores your account profile, incident reports, and backup metadata.
  • Cloudinary — stores the encrypted backup files and encrypted videos as private, access-controlled objects.
  • OpenStreetMap / Overpass — used to discover nearby hospitals, clinics, police, and fire stations. Your coordinates are sent through our backend to the Overpass service.

These services run under their own privacy policies, and we limit what is shared with each to the minimum needed.

6. Sharing Your Information

Rakshyaa does not sell your personal information. We share information only in these cases:

  • With your emergency contacts — the app prepares SMS messages and calls, but you send them yourself through your messaging and phone apps. The app does not message contacts automatically.
  • With emergency services — when you call emergency numbers (for example 112) from the app, your call and any details you choose to disclose go to those services.
  • With our safety operators — active incident reports (location and time) can be viewed by Rakshyaa operators through an internal portal. Operator response is not guaranteed and is not a replacement for emergency services.
  • With our service providers — Cloudflare and Cloudinary process data only to run the service, as described above.
  • To comply with law — where required by law, legal process, or to protect rights and safety.

7. Data Retention

Your account profile, incident reports, and encrypted backups are kept while your account is active. Deleting a video in the app removes it from your device. A backed-up copy may remain until you delete your account. Deleting your account in the app removes your profile, incidents, and backups from our servers. Our hosting providers may keep their own short-term logs and backups.

8. Your Privacy Rights

  • Access — request a summary of the data we hold.
  • Correction — update your profile data in-app at any time.
  • Deletion — use "Delete account and data" in the app's Profile and Settings screen, or email us to request deletion.
  • Withdrawing consent — stop location sharing, tracking, and recording at any time from the app.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at rakshyaapp@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

Rakshyaa is intended for people who need personal safety support and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We use strong technical safeguards: AES-256-GCM on-device encryption with keys in the Android Keystore, HTTPS for all network communication, server-side verification of Google ID tokens, and signed session tokens. Backup contents are encrypted before upload, so they stay unreadable even if our storage were compromised. Profile and incident data on our servers is protected by access controls but is not end-to-end encrypted.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be announced through the app or on rakshyaapp.github.io.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact: rakshyaapp@gmail.com.
